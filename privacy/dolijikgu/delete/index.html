Doljikgu Dashboard Data Deletion Instructions

Effective October 25, 2026

Doljikgu Dashboard (돌직구 대시보드, "the app") is an internal analytics tool operated by Law Office Hound (법률사무소 하운드). To ask us to delete your data held by the app, such as comments you left or direct messages you sent, follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to lawymer@daum.net.
  2. Include the following:
    1. The platform (Instagram, YouTube or TikTok)
    2. Your username or name
    3. What you want deleted (for example, a comment you left on a specific post)
  3. After verifying your request, we will delete the data from the app and let you know by email.

Note

If you delete your comment or message on Instagram, YouTube or TikTok yourself, the app will not collect it again from the next collection onward. To also remove data the app has already stored, please send a request as described above.
